# Manager Buddy: concept demo (about 2 minutes)

**What it is:** an AI teammate that works next to the FloLabs CRM, on its own and around the clock. You can also talk to it by chat or voice. It acts with your CRM permissions and asks a person before anything new reaches an applicant.

**Who it's for:** the FloLabs recruiting team, shown to colleagues and managers, technical or not.

**Angle:** the CRM chatbot answers when asked; Buddy works when nobody is asking. Half the video shows Buddy working alone: a night shift, then a task it finishes over five days.

**Hook:** the real size of the pipeline (42,975 people, 30,048 email threads, 25 stages), then "Who follows up on all of it?"

**Punchline:** "Works on its own, around the clock. Nothing in the CRM changes."

**Tone:** polished, calm, confident. Long holds so people can read, soft staggered transitions. The night shift dims the whole screen to night and brings it back at sunrise.

**Honesty:** Buddy isn't built yet. This is a scripted mock-up. Every person, company and number inside the app is a fictional example, and the corner tag says "Concept demo · example data".

## Sound

A soft 96 BPM track in A minor (Am, F, C, G). During the night shift the drums drop out and the plucks get quieter and longer, then the beat returns at sunrise. UI sounds are short plucks on the same chord tones, under the music: soft ticks for log entries, a low note for failures, a chime for the interested lead, a bell at 08:00, and a soft two-note "done" when the task completes.
